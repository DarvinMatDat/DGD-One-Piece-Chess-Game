How to play

Move: click one of your pieces, then a highlighted destination. White moves first.

Goal: checkmate the other king. A king in check must be protected, moved, or the attacker captured.

Character key: Luffy is king, Zoro queen, Brook knight, Chopper pawns. Nami is on light-square bishops and Usopp on dark-square bishops; Jinbei is on light-square rooks and Franky on dark-square rooks.

Special moves: castle by moving the king two squares; en passant is available immediately after a two-square pawn move; promote a pawn reaching the far edge to queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

Draws: stalemate, threefold repetition, 50 moves by each player without a pawn move or capture, or insufficient mating material.

Standard chess piece and special-move rules, with classroom-friendly automatic draw handling. Moves are made by clicking; use Undo to take back the last move.
